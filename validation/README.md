# Validation

Four stages, each with a question, a reference, an acceptance number fixed before the run, and
a verdict reported whether met or not. The applied study inherits the closure, the mesh rule, the
inflow treatment and the turbulent Schmidt number from here.

| stage | question | reference | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| [V0](V0/) | does the ABL survive an empty domain | analytical profiles | met (U within 2 %, k within 4 %) |
| [V1](V1/) | wake and roof separation of an isolated building | AIJ Case H wind tunnel | velocity hit rate 0.60 of 0.66, not met, grid-independent |
| [V2](V2/) | plume from a rooftop stack | IRSST R-848 wind tunnel | met at M = 1, not met at M = 3 |
| [V2b](V2b/) | does the model-scale result hold at full scale | V2 scaled 1:200 | met (3 %) |

Cases are regenerated by the scripts in each folder; meshes, fields and logs are not committed.
Metrics are computed by the `metrics.py` of each stage from the raw sample files and written to
`results/<stage>/`.
