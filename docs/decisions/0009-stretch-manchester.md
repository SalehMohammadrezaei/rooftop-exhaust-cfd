# 0009 Stretch item: real geometry demonstration (Manchester campus)

Date: 2026-10-12. Noted; not part of the MVP or the finished version.

After the applied data-hall study is complete, a separately labelled demonstration may apply the
validated method to real geometry: the University of Manchester Engineering Building (Nancy
Rothwell Building) and its neighbours, built from public sources (Ordnance Survey footprints with
OS MasterMap heights via the university's Digimap licence, Environment Agency open LiDAR, or
OpenStreetMap through Blender). Purpose: show geometry handling and presentation on a real site.
Stated limits, following Project.md section 2: illustrative only, no claim about real air quality
or comfort, no validation data for the site. It replaces the brief's "second identical hall
upwind" stretch option if one stretch is taken.

Presentation standard for all deliverables (2026-10-12): high quality, consistent figures
produced by script (ParaView Python and matplotlib), one visual style, every figure with its
numbers in a table beside it, README that states the question and the answer.
