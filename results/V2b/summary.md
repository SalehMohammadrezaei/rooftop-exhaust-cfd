# V2b summary (2026-10-12): the V2 isolated-building stack cases at full scale (x200)

Criterion (Project.md section 5): dimensionless dilution within the V2 uncertainty band of the
model-scale result (tunnel repeatability 15 % plus digitising 2.6 %, taken as 18 %). Cases: the two
M = 1 cases the model represents (decision 0008). Base mesh rule, 2500 iterations, Sct 0.4.

| Case | scale alone (full scale with the tunnel inflow scaled x200) | inflow alone (applied ABL against tunnel inflow, both full scale) | FAC2 vs tunnel: model / full tunnel-inflow / full applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| hs 1 m, M 1 | geometric mean ratio 1.03, range 1.01 to 1.04, 10/10 receptors within 18 % | 0.57 | 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.8 |
| hs 3 m, M 1 | 1.02, range 0.99 to 1.03, 10/10 within 18 % | 0.60 | 0.8 / 0.8 / 0.8 |

Scale check: MET. Running the case 200 times larger with the same dimensionless inflow changes the
dilution by 3 percent, well inside the band; Reynolds-number independence holds for this
sharp-edged geometry from Re 31,000 (tunnel) to 6 million (full scale).

Inflow sensitivity, not a pass/fail item: the applied ABL (z0 0.3 m, 15 % turbulence intensity at
roof height, rough ground, fixed-shear top) gives about 40 percent less roof dilution than the
tunnel's inflow (22 % intensity, smooth floor). The applied inflow is the project's; the effect
means the applied results spread plumes less along the roof than the tunnel would, i.e. they are
conservative in the direction of higher concentrations. Carried as the inflow part of the
uncertainty band (Project.md section 7). Files: results/V2b/*.json; cases under
~/scratch/openfoam_cases/V2b, generated by validation/V2b/make_cases.py.
