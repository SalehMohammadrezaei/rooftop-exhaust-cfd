# First pass: zero-thickness parapet (2026-10-12 to 13)

The four conditions run overnight on the original geometry, where the parapet was a baffle
(decision 0012 replaced it with a 0.5 m solid ring). Kept as the record of the first pass; the
0-degree 10 m/s case is the comparison point for the geometry change. The 60, 90 and 0/3 cases
stopped their tracer stage at 1500 iterations and are not converged (amplitude 18 to 26 %).
